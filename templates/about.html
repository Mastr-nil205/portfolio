About / The person behind the work

Rooted in Haiti.
Always looking ahead.

I’m Luigi Josue, an IT professional and technical builder based in Indianapolis, originally from Cap-Haïtien, Haiti.

One connected story.

My interests sit at the intersection of technology and practical problem-solving. Supporting people at work, understanding how systems connect, and building software are different ways to explore that same curiosity.

Professional support work keeps me close to the people using technology. Personal projects give me room to investigate new tools and learn through implementation.

Two places.
A broader perspective.

My background in Haiti and professional development in the United States shape the questions I’m interested in: how technology works, who can access it, and what makes it useful.

I value independence, clear thinking, and the patience to work through a problem carefully.

A student of the problem.

My independent learning includes Python, web development, Linux, virtualization, and self-hosted systems. I distinguish that exploration from professional production experience.

This website is a record of my technical work and professional journey, with space to document what I learn along the way.
